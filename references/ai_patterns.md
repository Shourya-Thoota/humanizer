# AI Writing Patterns: Full Catalog

This preserved compact catalog adapts [Wikipedia's field observations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing), not a validated detector. Read `writing-quality-gate.md` for governing rules and `writing-evidence.md` for scoped research. Model-era labels are historical guide observations, not verified frequency claims for all models.

Examples sometimes assume additional facts, numbers, sources, or opinions absent from their “before” text. They illustrate a possible form only if those details are independently supplied and verified; otherwise do not use the “after.” Preserve negation, uncertainty, safety limits, and actual voice even when a legacy example does not.

## Content Patterns

### 1. Undue Emphasis on Significance, Legacy, and Broader Trends

**Words to watch:** stands/serves as, is a testament/reminder, a vital/significant/crucial/pivotal/key role/moment, underscores/highlights its importance/significance, reflects broader, symbolizing its ongoing/enduring/lasting, contributing to the, setting the stage for, marking/shaping the, represents/marks a shift, key turning point, evolving landscape, focal point, indelible mark, deeply rooted, distinction matters/is important because

**Problem:** LLMs puff up importance by adding statements about how arbitrary aspects represent or contribute to a broader topic.

**Fix:** Remove the significance framing. State facts directly. If something truly is significant, show it through specifics, not announcement.

**Before:** The Statistical Institute of Catalonia was officially established in 1989, marking a pivotal moment in the evolution of regional statistics in Spain.
**After:** The Statistical Institute of Catalonia was established in 1989 to collect and publish regional statistics independently from Spain's national statistics office.

---

### 2. Canned Emphasis on Notability and Media Coverage

**Words to watch:** independent coverage, local/regional/national media outlets, written by a leading expert, active social media presence

**Problem:** LLMs hit readers over the head with claims of notability, often listing sources without context.

**Fix:** Replace source-name-dropping with specific, attributed claims from those sources.

**Before:** Her views have been cited in The New York Times, BBC, Financial Times, and The Hindu. She maintains an active social media presence with over 500,000 followers.
**After:** In a 2024 New York Times interview, she argued that AI regulation should focus on outcomes rather than methods.

---

### 3. Superficial Analyses with -ing Endings

**Words to watch:** highlighting/underscoring/emphasizing..., ensuring..., reflecting/symbolizing..., contributing to..., cultivating/fostering..., encompassing..., showcasing..., enhancing..., valuable insights, align/resonate with

**Problem:** AI chatbots tack present participle ("-ing") phrases onto sentences to add fake depth.

**Fix:** Remove the superficial analysis or replace with specific, attributed statements.

**Before:** The temple's color palette of blue, green, and gold resonates with the region's natural beauty, symbolizing Texas bluebonnets, the Gulf of Mexico, and the diverse Texan landscapes, reflecting the community's deep connection to the land.
**After:** The temple uses blue, green, and gold colors. The architect said these were chosen to reference local bluebonnets and the Gulf coast.

---

### 4. Promotional and Advertisement-like Language

**Words to watch:** boasts a, vibrant, rich (figurative), profound, enhancing its, showcasing, exemplifies, commitment to, natural beauty, nestled, in the heart of, groundbreaking (figurative), renowned, breathtaking, must-visit, stunning, featuring, diverse array

**Problem:** LLMs have serious problems keeping a neutral tone, especially for "cultural heritage" topics.

**Fix:** Replace promotional adjectives with specific, verifiable facts.

**Before:** Nestled within the breathtaking region of Gonder in Ethiopia, Alamata Raya Kobo stands as a vibrant town with a rich cultural heritage and stunning natural beauty.
**After:** Alamata Raya Kobo is a town in the Gonder region of Ethiopia, known for its weekly market and 18th-century church.

---

### 5. Vague Expression of Connection or Association

**Words to watch:** has been associated with, has been linked to, connected to, tied to, related to, involved in

**Problem:** LLMs use vague connectors when they can't establish a specific relationship.

**Fix:** Specify the actual relationship, or remove if unsupported.

**Before:** The project has been associated with several international organizations.
**After:** The project received funding from the World Bank and partnered with three local NGOs.

---

### 6. Vague Attributions and Overgeneralization of Opinions

**Words to watch:** critics argue, some say, many believe, experts note, observers contend, proponents claim, detractors suggest

**Problem:** Unattributed generalizations that sound authoritative but cite no one.

**Fix:** Name specific people or sources, or state the opinion directly if it's the author's.

**Before:** Critics argue that the policy fails to address underlying structural issues.
**After:** In a 2023 Brookings Institution report, economist Sarah Chen argued the policy fails to address underlying structural issues.

---

### 7. Outline-like Conclusions About Challenges and Future Prospects

**Words to watch:** Despite its... faces several challenges..., Despite these challenges, Challenges and Legacy, Future Outlook

**Problem:** Rigid formulaic conclusions, especially sections beginning "Despite its [positive words], [subject] faces challenges..."

**Fix:** Remove the rigid structure. If challenges exist, describe them specifically without the template framing.

**Before:** Despite its innovative approach and dedicated team, the startup faces several challenges, including limited funding, regulatory hurdles, and fierce competition from established players.
**After:** The startup has a strong team and a novel product, but it's also competing against companies with ten times its funding and decades of regulatory relationships.

## Language and Grammar

### 8. High Density of "AI Vocabulary" Words

**Era-specific vocabulary to watch:**
- **2023-2024 (GPT-4 era):** Additionally, boasts, bolstered, crucial, delve, emphasizing, enduring, garner, intricate/intricacies, interplay, key, landscape, meticulously, pivotal, underscore, tapestry, testament, valuable, vibrant
- **2024-2025 (GPT-4o era):** align with, bolstered, crucial, emphasizing, enhance, enduring, fostering, highlighting, pivotal, showcasing, underscore, vibrant
- **2025-2026 (GPT-5 era):** emphasizing, enhance, highlighting, showcasing

**Fix:** Replace with simpler, more specific alternatives.
- delve → explore, examine, look into
- tapestry → mix, combination, range
- landscape → scene, field, area
- crucial → important, key, critical (if truly warranted)
- underscore → highlight, show, emphasize
- testament → example, evidence, sign
- vibrant → lively, active, colorful
- intricate → complex, detailed
- foster → encourage, support, develop
- leverage → use, take advantage of
- utilize → use

---

### 9. Avoidance of Basic Copulatives ("is"/"are" Phrases)

**Problem:** AI copyedits often replace simple "is/are" constructions with more complex phrasing.

**Fix:** Simple "is/are" is often clearer and more direct. Don't force complexity.

**Before:** The organization refers to a nonprofit founded in 2010.
**After:** The organization is a nonprofit founded in 2010.

---

### 10. Negative Parallelisms

**Pattern A: Not just X, but also Y**

**Before:** The project is not just about technology, but also about community empowerment.
**After:** The project combines technology with community empowerment.

**Pattern B: Not X, but Y**

**Before:** This is not a limitation, but an opportunity for growth.
**After:** This limitation creates an opportunity for growth.

**Pattern C: No X. No Y. Just Z.**

**Before:** No fluff. No filler. Just results.
**After:** We focus on results and skip the unnecessary extras.

---

### 11. Excessive Conjunctive Phrases

**Words to watch:** Furthermore, Moreover, Additionally, Consequently, Thus, Hence, In conclusion, In summary, Overall

**Problem:** Overused at sentence beginnings to create false sophistication.

**Fix:** Remove or replace with conversational transitions: "Also," "So," "That means," "The result is," or just start with the idea.

## Style and Structure

### 12. Rule of Three (Tricolons)

**Problem:** AI exhibits extreme fixation on triplets for rhetorical effect.

**Before:** The company is innovative, creative, and forward-thinking.
**After:** The company experiments with new approaches.

**Before:** No family. No calls. Just silence.
**After:** She was completely alone.

---

### 13. Em Dash Overuse

**Problem:** Too many clause interruptions can obstruct reading. Neither using nor avoiding em dashes establishes authorship.

**Fix:** Use em dashes sparingly — for genuine emphasis or interruption, not as a default punctuation.

---

### 14. Uniform Sentence Length and Structure

**Problem:** AI output often has sentences of similar length and structure, creating monotonous rhythm.

**Fix:** Repair distracting monotony while letting ideas determine sentence length; do not manufacture burstiness.

---

### 15. Overuse of Boldface and Formatting

**Problem:** Excessive bold text, especially in body paragraphs or for emphasis that doesn't need it.

**Fix:** Use formatting for genuine structural emphasis only. Let words carry weight.

---

## Communication Artifacts

### 16. Hedging Disclaimers

**Words to watch:** It's important to note, It's crucial to remember, It should be noted that, Always check before you use something

**Problem:** AI includes didactic disclaimers and cautionary framing, often advising an imagined reader.

**Fix:** Remove redundant framing, not genuine uncertainty, evidentiary limits, or safety/legal guidance.

---

### 17. Chatbot Correspondence Artifacts

**Words to watch:** a Wikipedia-style article, I hope this helps, Of course!, Certainly!, You're absolutely right!, Would you like..., is there anything else, let me know, more detailed breakdown, here is a...

**Problem:** Text pasted from AI chatbot conversation rather than intended as final content.

**Fix:** Remove all conversational framing and meta-commentary.

---

### 18. Canned User Page Format

**Problem (user profiles/bios):** Rigid formats with sections like "About Me," "My Interests," "Let's Connect!" with emoji and bold text.

**Fix:** Replace with natural prose or a simpler structure appropriate to the platform.

## Historical Indicators

### 19. Section Summaries / "In Conclusion"

**Problem:** Older LLMs added "Conclusion" sections or ended paragraphs by restating core ideas.

**Fix:** Remove summary sections unless genuinely needed. Trust readers to remember what they just read.

**Before:** In conclusion, the policy represents a significant step forward, though it faces notable implementation challenges that will require sustained attention from stakeholders across multiple sectors.
**After:** The policy changes the rules, but getting it to work on the ground will take longer than the press releases suggest.

---

### 20. Prompt Refusal Artifacts

**Words to watch:** As an AI language model, As a large language model, I cannot offer medical advice, but I can..., I'm sorry...

**Problem:** Outright refusals have become rare but may appear in older content.

**Fix:** Remove only pasted boilerplate that does not belong in the deliverable. Preserve required refusals, safety boundaries, and substantive limitations.

**Before:** As an AI language model, I cannot provide legal advice, but I can offer some general information about the process.
**After:** Here's how the process typically works. For specific legal questions, consult a qualified attorney.

## Summary Checklist

When humanizing, scan for these top-priority tells:

- [ ] Overused AI vocabulary (delve, tapestry, landscape, crucial, underscore)
- [ ] Significance inflation (testament to, pivotal moment, reflects broader)
- [ ] Superficial -ing analyses (highlighting, symbolizing, ensuring)
- [ ] Promotional puffery (vibrant, stunning, nestled, breathtaking)
- [ ] Vague attributions (critics argue, some say, many believe)
- [ ] Negative parallelisms (not just X but Y, no X no Y just Z)
- [ ] Excessive transitions (furthermore, moreover, additionally)
- [ ] Rule of three triplets
- [ ] Uniform sentence rhythm
- [ ] Hedging disclaimers (it's important to note)
