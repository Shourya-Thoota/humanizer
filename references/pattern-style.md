## Style patterns

Apply these as contextual editorial examples, not authorship judgments. The field-guide provenance is [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing); useful formatting should follow reader needs, as explained by [Digital.gov](https://digital.gov/guides/plain-language/design). Do not add facts or delete qualifications merely to imitate an “after.”

### 17. Em dashes and punctuation monotony

**Problem:** Dense interruptions can make a sentence harder to follow. Edit punctuation for clarity and the user's preference, not a quota. Neither the presence nor absence of em dashes proves authorship; do not impose artificial variety.

**Before:**
> The term is primarily promoted by Dutch institutions—not by the people themselves. You don't say "Netherlands, Europe" as an address—yet this mislabeling continues—even in official documents.

**After:**
> The term is primarily promoted by Dutch institutions, not by the people themselves. You don't say "Netherlands, Europe" as an address, yet this mislabeling continues in official documents.


### 18. Uniform short sentences

**Problem:** Repeated short sentence shapes can make prose choppy. No universal sentence-length range identifies model-written text; fix the reader's actual difficulty.

**Fix:** Let a sentence run long when the thought is long, and keep short ones where they land. Join choppy runs with "and," "but," "because," or "which."

**Before:**
> The update fixes the login bug. It also speeds up search. Users reported the bug last month. The team prioritized the fix. It is live now.

**After:**
> The team prioritized the login bug users reported last month. The update is now live: it fixes that bug and speeds up search.


### 19. Title case in headings

**Problem:** AI chatbots capitalize all main words in headings.

**Before:**
> ## Strategic Negotiations And Global Partnerships

**After:**
> ## Strategic negotiations and global partnerships


### 20. Overuse of boldface

**Problem:** AI chatbots emphasize phrases in boldface mechanically, a habit picked up from READMEs, slide decks, and listicles. A common version bolds every instance of a chosen term, "key takeaways" style.

**Before:**
> It blends **OKRs (Objectives and Key Results)**, **KPIs (Key Performance Indicators)**, and visual strategy tools such as the **Business Model Canvas (BMC)** and **Balanced Scorecard (BSC)**.

**After:**
> It blends OKRs, KPIs, and visual strategy tools like the Business Model Canvas and Balanced Scorecard.


### 21. Inline-header vertical lists

**Problem:** AI outputs lists where items start with bolded headers followed by colons. Variants: bullets typed as •, -, or – characters; numbers typed as "1."; or the bold header with no colon at all.

**Before:**
> - **User Experience:** The user experience has been significantly improved with a new interface.
> - **Performance:** Performance has been enhanced through optimized algorithms.
> - **Security:** Security has been strengthened with end-to-end encryption.

**After:**
> The update improves the interface, speeds up load times through optimized algorithms, and adds end-to-end encryption.


### 22. Heading structure errors

**Signs to watch:**
- A heading at the very top that repeats the document's own title.
- Skipped levels, jumping straight to third-level headings.
- Every section as a top-level heading.
- A heading with nothing under it except more headings.
- A horizontal rule (---) between every section.

**Fix:** One title, a real hierarchy, some text under each heading, and rules only where there's a real break.


### 23. Emojis

**Problem:** AI chatbots often decorate headings or bullet points with emojis. Current models do this less, but it still shows up, especially in bios and comments. Watch for canned profile pages with sections like "About Me," "My Interests," and "Let's Connect!" decorated with emoji and bold.

**Before:**
> 🚀 **Launch Phase:** The product launches in Q3
> 💡 **Key Insight:** Users prefer simplicity
> ✅ **Next Steps:** Schedule follow-up meeting

**After:**
> The product launches in Q3. User research showed a preference for simplicity. Next step: schedule a follow-up meeting.


### 24. Small, unneeded tables

**Problem:** Some AIs build tiny, minimally formatted tables (two columns, three rows) for information that reads better as a sentence.

**Fix:** Use prose only if it improves comprehension. A small table can be useful for comparison or scanning.


### 25. Curly quotation marks and apostrophes

**Problem:** Inconsistent typography can distract, but curly or straight quotation marks do not establish authorship. Preserve exact quotations and code; regularize surrounding prose only when appropriate.

**Fix:** Make the marks consistent, in whichever style the writer's draft mostly uses. Don't convert a person's smart quotes just because they're curly.

**Before:**
> He said “the project is on track" but others didn’t agree.

**After:**
> He said "the project is on track" but others didn't agree.
