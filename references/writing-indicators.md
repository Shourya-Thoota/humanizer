# Writing indicators and remedies

Ninety contextual review prompts across 15 categories. Use the relevant category for a local problem or the full catalog for an explicitly comprehensive audit. These are editorial checks, not 90 proven AI markers, a scoring system, or a ban list. Leave a passage alone when it works for its reader and purpose.

The evidence and its limitations are in `writing-evidence.md`. Categories adapt observed patterns and reader-centered guidance; individual examples and remedies below are editorial judgments, not experimental findings.

## Substance and specificity

These checks operationalize the difference between useful information and portable praise described in [Wikipedia's field guide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing).

| ID | Review question | Remedy and exception |
|---|---|---|
| W01 | Could this sentence describe almost any topic? | Add supplied specifics or cut it; a genuine general principle may remain. |
| W02 | Does “pivotal,” “transformative,” or similar praise replace a consequence? | State the demonstrated change; preserve attributed opinions. |
| W03 | Is a concrete mechanism replaced by “drives,” “enables,” or “fosters”? | Explain how if evidence supports it; do not invent a mechanism. |
| W04 | Are abstract benefits repeated without an example or decision? | Use relevant supplied evidence, not fabricated detail. |
| W05 | Does the conclusion merely restate the heading? | Develop or remove it; instructional repetition may be intentional. |
| W06 | Is there a promise of practical advice but no usable action? | Supply an actionable step within scope or narrow the promise. |

## Reasoning and argument

These are editorial reasoning checks; fluent prose cannot substitute for a valid inference.

| ID | Review question | Remedy and exception |
|---|---|---|
| W07 | Does “therefore” connect claims without an actual inference? | Show the missing step or remove the claim of implication. |
| W08 | Does an “-ing” tail assert importance without support? | Explain the link or cut it; preserve genuine causal explanation. |
| W09 | Is correlation rewritten as causation? | Restore the source's evidentiary strength. |
| W10 | Are pros and cons forced into equal weight despite unequal evidence? | Represent evidence and uncertainty proportionately. |
| W11 | Does a confident conclusion exceed the examples supplied? | Narrow scope or request evidence. |
| W12 | Does a rhetorical question evade the actual answer? | Answer directly unless suspense serves the requested genre. |

## Claims and sourcing

Plausible citations may be false or fail to support the surrounding claim; checking style is not checking references. ([Citation study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-023-41032-5))

| ID | Review question | Remedy and exception |
|---|---|---|
| W13 | Does “experts say” conceal missing attribution? | Name a verified source or flag the unresolved claim. |
| W14 | Does a citation exist but support a weaker claim? | Restore supported wording, not merely a working URL. |
| W15 | Has a number lost its date, denominator, range, or unit? | Restore the source's context. |
| W16 | Does a source-handling disclaimer repeat an obvious limitation? | Cut repetition only; keep substantive limitations. |
| W17 | Is fame inferred from a list of press mentions? | Explain relevant documented significance without self-validating notability. |
| W18 | Does an invented quote, DOI, study, or testimonial make the text vivid? | Remove/flag the fabrication; never guess replacement evidence. |

## Meaning preservation

| ID | Review question | Remedy and exception |
|---|---|---|
| W19 | Did an edit remove “not,” “unless,” or another condition? | Restore the logical boundary. |
| W20 | Did “may” become “will,” or “should” become “must”? | Restore intended certainty or obligation. |
| W21 | Did “some” become “all,” or a pilot become a general result? | Restore the quantified scope. |
| W22 | Was the actor or attribution changed by a cleaner sentence? | Restore who did or said what. |
| W23 | Was an inconvenient caveat or negative result omitted? | Restore substantive content even if it complicates the story. |
| W24 | Did a synonym change a defined legal/technical term? | Reuse the correct term consistently. |

## Rhetorical templates

Recurring templates merit review in context, not automatic deletion. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Signs_of_AI_writing), [UZH](https://www.uzh.ch/blog/ub/2026/09/10/schreibe-ich-schon-wie-eine-ki/?lang=en))

| ID | Review question | Remedy and exception |
|---|---|---|
| W25 | Does “not X, but Y” create a false contrast? | State Y directly; retain real distinctions. |
| W26 | Are three-item lists forced despite two or four real items? | Let the content determine the count. |
| W27 | Is “from X to Y” a meaningless range? | Name the actual coverage or endpoints. |
| W28 | Do “No X. No Y. Just Z.” fragments repeat mechanically? | State the real difference without theatrical denial. |
| W29 | Does a colon reveal add drama but no information? | Write the claim directly; keep useful labels and colons. |
| W30 | Are “challenges and opportunities” sections generic inserts? | Include only actual challenges, tradeoffs, or opportunities. |

## Sentence construction

Model and genre differences argue against universal grammar rules. ([PNAS](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2422455122), [Genre study](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.14111v1))

| ID | Review question | Remedy and exception |
|---|---|---|
| W31 | Do nominalizations obscure the action? | Use a verb where clearer; preserve useful technical nouns. |
| W32 | Are several participial clauses hanging from one assertion? | Separate distinct claims or clarify their relationship. |
| W33 | Do noun stacks make referents hard to parse? | Unpack only the ambiguous stack. |
| W34 | Does passive voice conceal a necessary actor? | Name the actor if known; keep legitimate actorless passive. |
| W35 | Is “serves as” or “boasts” replacing a sufficient “is” or “has”? | Use the plain relation unless nuance would be lost. |
| W36 | Does coordination cram unrelated points into one sentence? | Split at a real conceptual boundary, not a word quota. |

## Vocabulary and precision

Lexical shifts can be real at corpus level without making a word invalid in an individual sentence. ([Kobak and colleagues](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.07016))

| ID | Review question | Remedy and exception |
|---|---|---|
| W37 | Do fashionable words cluster without adding precision? | Prefer the exact word, including the fashionable one when correct. |
| W38 | Does synonym cycling make one entity seem like several? | Keep terminology stable. |
| W39 | Are modifiers such as “truly” or “incredibly” doing no work? | Remove empty intensifiers; preserve intentional emotion. |
| W40 | Does jargon obscure a simple concept for this audience? | Explain or replace it; retain expert vocabulary for experts. |
| W41 | Does “associated with” hide an unknown relationship? | Specify if known; preserve genuine statistical association. |
| W42 | Does inflated phrasing replace an ordinary verb? | Prefer the ordinary verb when equally precise. |

## Rhythm and repetition

| ID | Review question | Remedy and exception |
|---|---|---|
| W43 | Do successive sentences repeat the same syntactic frame? | Vary only where monotony impedes reading; keep intentional parallelism. |
| W44 | Are all paragraphs forced to the same length? | Organize by ideas, not visual symmetry. |
| W45 | Are short fragments stacked to simulate conviction? | Restore the relationship between claims. |
| W46 | Is sentence length mechanically alternated? | Let complexity determine length; no burstiness target. |
| W47 | Is the same point paraphrased repeatedly? | Keep the strongest useful statement; preserve necessary reinforcement. |
| W48 | Does each paragraph start with the same transition? | Use actual logical relationships, often without a transition word. |

## Openings, transitions, and endings

| ID | Review question | Remedy and exception |
|---|---|---|
| W49 | Does “In today's rapidly changing world” delay the subject? | Start with the real issue unless historical context matters. |
| W50 | Does the answer announce what it will do instead of doing it? | Cut unneeded staging; keep navigation for long documents. |
| W51 | Does a heading, opening line, and first bullet all say the same thing? | Remove redundant framing. |
| W52 | Does “Ultimately” introduce a generic moral? | End on the actual conclusion, consequence, or next step. |
| W53 | Is a conclusion a full recap of a very short answer? | Stop once complete; preserve summaries in long work. |
| W54 | Is a follow-up offer unrelated or formulaic? | Omit unless useful or required by higher-priority instructions. |

## Structure and visual presentation

Headings, lists, tables, and emphasis can improve comprehension when meaning determines their use. ([Digital.gov design](https://digital.gov/guides/plain-language/design))

| ID | Review question | Remedy and exception |
|---|---|---|
| W55 | Is every sentence under a new heading? | Group related points unless the format requires discrete fields. |
| W56 | Is every bullet a bold label plus a vague explanation? | Use specific labels and real distinctions, or prose. |
| W57 | Is most of the page bold? | Emphasize only genuine priorities. |
| W58 | Does a table force incomparable items into empty cells? | Use prose or a meaningful comparison structure. |
| W59 | Do emoji or title-case headings conflict with the channel? | Follow the user's register, brand, and accessibility needs. |
| W60 | Has formatting been removed merely because it looks “AI”? | Restore structure that aids scanning and comprehension. |

## Voice, stance, and relationship

Preserve the writer's stance as well as literal meaning; assistance can alter readers' perceptions of the author. ([Persona study](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.22503v1))

| ID | Review question | Remedy and exception |
|---|---|---|
| W61 | Has an opinion become a neutral corporate paraphrase? | Restore the author's approved stance. |
| W62 | Has uncertainty become performative confidence? | Keep the original degree of confidence. |
| W63 | Does a routine message acquire excessive warmth or apology? | Fit the relationship and purpose; retain culturally appropriate courtesy. |
| W64 | Is the response flattering instead of useful? | Engage with the substance; agree or disagree on reasons. |
| W65 | Has professionalism erased humor, bluntness, or dialect? | Preserve intentional voice unless asked to change register. |
| W66 | Does every message sound like customer support? | Use the actual context rather than generic service language. |

## Culture, language, and audience

Preserve cultural detail without stereotyping; homogenization findings are bounded by the studied populations. ([Cultural study](https://arxiv.org/html/2409.11360v3))

| ID | Review question | Remedy and exception |
|---|---|---|
| W67 | Has regional English silently become American English? | Retain the supplied variety or requested target. |
| W68 | Were meaningful names, foods, practices, or places generalized away? | Restore relevant supplied details. |
| W69 | Is an author's own culture explained as exotic scenery? | Respect their perspective and intended reader. |
| W70 | Were honorifics, apologies, or indirect requests stripped indiscriminately? | Preserve relationship-sensitive conventions. |
| W71 | Was an expert passage simplified below its reader's needs? | Keep necessary complexity and define only what is needed. |
| W72 | Is a nonnative phrasing judged suspicious just because it is unfamiliar? | Assess clarity and intent, not presumed authorship. |

## Fabricated humanity

These checks prevent false personalization rather than diagnose an author's origin.

| ID | Review question | Remedy and exception |
|---|---|---|
| W73 | Does “I remember” invent a lived experience? | Remove it unless supplied by the user or explicitly fictional. |
| W74 | Were opinions or feelings added to make the writer sound alive? | Obtain approval or preserve the original stance. |
| W75 | Were random typos or grammar mistakes inserted? | Remove artificial errors; keep intentional dialect and exact quotes. |
| W76 | Is slang or profanity imposed on an otherwise formal writer? | Match actual voice, not a generic idea of humanity. |
| W77 | Are confessions such as “honestly” or “I won't lie” repetitive tics? | Keep only when they express a real intended contrast or stance. |
| W78 | Are tangents and rough edges manufactured as camouflage? | Keep only material serving meaning, characterization, or voice. |

## Output artifacts and task fit

| ID | Review question | Remedy and exception |
|---|---|---|
| W79 | Does ready-to-send copy include “Here's your revised version”? | Return the copy alone unless commentary was requested. |
| W80 | Are prompt fragments, tool tokens, or internal notes visible? | Remove actual leaks; preserve intentionally quoted examples. |
| W81 | Are placeholders left in supposedly final copy? | Fill from known facts or flag what is missing. |
| W82 | Did an edit alter quoted testimony, code, or formulas? | Restore exact spans unless explicitly asked to change them. |
| W83 | Is the output far longer than its practical purpose? | Cut scope and framing without omitting necessary substance. |
| W84 | Did an audit become a rewrite, or a rewrite become a lecture? | Restore the requested output mode. |

## Audit discipline and uncertainty

Detector behavior varies by tool, corpus, language, and era; opposing findings prevent a universal claim. ([English pilot](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.02819), [Czech study](https://aclanthology.org/2026.eacl-srw.20/))

| ID | Review question | Remedy and exception |
|---|---|---|
| W85 | Is a single word or punctuation mark being treated as proof? | Describe the concrete writing issue, not authorship. |
| W86 | Is an aggregate statistic applied to one person's text? | Restore the study's unit of analysis and uncertainty. |
| W87 | Is an old model's behavior described as universal today? | Date and scope the claim; refresh only when needed. |
| W88 | Is the rewrite optimized for an AI detector? | Optimize for meaning, usefulness, and authentic supplied voice instead. |
| W89 | Does passing a linter imply semantic correctness or reader preference? | Report only the mechanical checks actually performed. |
| W90 | Does “human” become a promise of origin, perfection, or undetectability? | State the bounded quality goal without misrepresenting authorship. |
